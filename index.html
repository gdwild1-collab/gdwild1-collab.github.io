About Me
One of the first CS hires
not the founder, but the builder

I'm a Seattle-based Customer Success leader who tends to get brought in to build things from scratch: the playbook, the process, the team culture, and then turn all of it into a real revenue engine. My work sits at the intersection of AI-powered SaaS, complex enterprise relationships, and steady, data-backed growth.

At NomadGo, I was one of the first enterprise CSMs hired at this early-stage AI startup. My job was to build the customer success function from the ground up, not the company itself, just the part that keeps customers happy and renewing. That meant writing the playbook, designing onboarding, and personally managing a $25M portfolio of global QSR and CPG brands. I left having kept 100% gross retention and 106% NRR.

Before that, I spent four years at Springbig, earning two promotions up to Senior Team Lead. I built a reputation there for retaining and growing strategic accounts, and for helping other CSMs learn to do the same.

I do my best work when there's an important customer relationship on the line, a new product that needs a champion, or a team that's ready to grow. I want to be somewhere that treats customer success as a real driver of revenue, not just a support line.
