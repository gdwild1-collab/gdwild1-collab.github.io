About Me
From startup founder to global enterprise
— I build CS that scales

I'm a Seattle-based Customer Success leader with a track record of building enterprise CS functions from the ground up and turning them into measurable revenue engines. My work sits at the intersection of AI-powered SaaS, complex enterprise relationships, and data-driven growth.

At NomadGo, I was hired as the first enterprise CSM to establish the entire CS function — writing the playbook, designing onboarding workflows, and personally managing a $25M portfolio of global QSR and CPG brands. I left with 100% gross retention and 106% NRR.

Before that, I spent four years at Springbig — rising through two promotions to Senior Team Lead — and built a reputation for retaining and growing strategic accounts while coaching others to do the same.

I'm at my best when there's a big customer relationship to protect, a new product to champion, or a team to develop. I thrive in high-ownership environments where CS is treated as a revenue function, not just a support ticket queue.
